Full and part loads · 44 t articulated curtainsider

Full loads, part loads, long distance.

Our Class 1 units pull 13.6 m curtainsider trailers for full and part loads, trunking and long-distance work. Curtain sides mean forklift loading from either side, so collections are quick and nothing is dragged across the deck.

  • Up to 26 palletson a 13.6 m trailer
  • Side loadingforklift access from either side

Pallet distribution · 18 t rigid curtainsider with tail-lift

Pallets, collected and delivered.

Quarter, half and full pallets on 18-tonne rigid curtainsiders. With a tail-lift and pump truck on board we deliver to addresses without a forklift, and proof of delivery is captured once the goods are signed for.

  • Tail-liftno forklift needed at delivery
  • Proof of deliverysigned for at the door

Same day and urgent · 3.5 t MAN TGE van

Same day, direct, on a dedicated van.

For consignments that cannot wait: a dedicated van, collected and driven straight to the delivery address with no depot in between, and signed for at the other end.

  • Directno depot, no transfers
  • Dedicated vanyour consignment only

Fleet standards · MAN TGX 6×2 tractor unit

Maintained to operator-licence standards.

Scheduled safety inspections, daily walk-round checks and a logged defect system keep the fleet on the road. Our HGV drivers hold the Driver CPC and drive on tachographs, as the law requires.

  • Inspected and checkedscheduled inspections, daily walk-rounds
  • Driver CPCevery HGV driver

Privacy notice

How we handle your details.

Who we are

ARM Transport, Yard 1, Excel Center, Preston Street, Manchester, M18 8DB. We are the data controller for any personal information collected through this website. You can contact us on 0161 706 0601, by email at info@armtransport.co.uk, or by post at the address above.

What we collect and why

When you request a quote we ask for your name, a phone number or email address, your company (optional), the collection and delivery locations, what is being moved and when it is needed. We use these details only to price the job, reply to you and, if you go ahead, arrange the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

How enquiries reach us

A quote request reaches us as an email from your own email app, or through a form-processing service acting on our behalf if we use one, and is then held in our email and business systems. If you call us, we may note down what you tell us so that we can quote accurately.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to answer the enquiry and, where work is carried out, for as long as our business and tax records require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
